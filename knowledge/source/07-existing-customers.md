# Existing customers

## Customer portal and account questions
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

Every SBG customer gets online portal access at https://app.sbgfunding.com/login showing the outstanding balance, payment history and renewal eligibility. For anything else, call 844-284-2725 to reach your Relationship Manager (Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Eastern). Account-specific questions such as balances, payoff amounts or payment changes are handled by your Relationship Manager or through the portal, because they require verifying your identity.

## How payments work
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

For revenue-based financing, the business bank account is debited automatically based on the amount and frequency in the agreement, so there is nothing to track or remember. Other products follow the repayment schedule set in the agreement, weekly or monthly.

## Paying off early
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

You can repay financing, an advance or a line of credit at any time. Depending on the agreement, you may qualify for full interest forgiveness or a discount on the balance. Many products have no prepayment penalty and some offer prepayment discounts. Check your customer agreement or ask your account manager.

## Renewals and additional funding
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

Most clients become eligible for additional financing once they have paid down 40% to 50% of their balance, sometimes sooner. Contact your Relationship Manager or call 844-284-2725. Line of credit customers can also request a limit increase as the business grows and shows consistent repayment.

## If revenue drops or a payment will be hard to make
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/resources/unlock-business-potential-with-mca-loans/

Talk to your provider early. If revenue drops, call before a payment is missed; it is much easier to adjust a schedule ahead of a problem than after one. Existing customers should contact their Relationship Manager at 844-284-2725 as soon as they anticipate difficulty. Budget around fixed daily or weekly payments, keep a cushion for payroll, rent and suppliers, and avoid stacking several advances from different providers.
