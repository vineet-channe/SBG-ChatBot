## What independent reviewers report
Source: https://www.bankrate.com/loans/small-business/reviews/sbg-funding

Summarized from third-party reviews (Bankrate, Business News Daily, Finder, LendingTree and ConsumerAffairs) as retrieved in October 2026. Third-party reviews of SBG Funding are generally positive but date-sensitive and sometimes inconsistent with each other. Bankrate's review (February 2026) scores SBG 4.6 out of 5. Reviewers praise the fast application, broad product range and customer service. Reported figures for minimum revenue, credit scores and loan limits differ between reviewers and from SBG's own current terms, and LendingTree notes that SBG quotes monthly or weekly rates rather than an APR, which makes comparisons harder. SBG's own published terms (500+ FICO, 6+ months, $150,000+ revenue, soft pull only) take precedence over third-party summaries, and any figure from a review should be confirmed with an SBG advisor.
