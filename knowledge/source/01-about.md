# About SBG Funding

## Company overview
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/about-us/

SBG Funding is a New York City-based small business financing company founded in March 2017. It is a direct provider of revenue-based financing and also runs a marketplace that places other products (term loans, lines of credit, SBA financing, equipment, invoice and bridge financing) with lending partners in its network. One application reaches the full range of business financing instead of whatever a single lender happens to offer.

Key facts published by SBG Funding:

- More than $3 billion in financing delivered since 2017.
- More than 28,000 U.S. small businesses funded across more than 64,000 transactions.
- Financing from $5,000 to $10,000,000, available in all 50 states.
- Inc. 5000 honoree five years in a row (2022 to 2026), ranked #778 in 2026.
- Great Place to Work certification (2022).
- Brand promise: "Capital That Keeps Up" - the money moves as fast as the business does.
- Founder and CEO: Jeffrey Sachs. Legal entity: Mission Capital LLC.

## How the SBG marketplace works
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/about-us/

SBG funds some products itself and arranges the rest through its lender network. The process has four parts:

- One application: apply once online with your four most recent business bank statements. There is no hard credit pull and no stack of paperwork.
- A dedicated advisor reviews it: an in-house SBG advisor (not an algorithm and not a call center) weighs revenue, cash flow, time in business and more than 50 other factors.
- You are matched to the right capital: when revenue-based financing fits best, SBG funds it directly; when a term loan, line of credit, equipment financing, invoice financing or SBA financing is the better tool, SBG places it with the right lender in its network.
- You get funded fast: decisions in as little as 24 hours and funding shortly after.

SBG is not a lead-generation site. Applicants are not passed to a call center or sold into a lead pool, and they keep one dedicated advisor for the life of their business.

## SBG compared with banks and typical online lenders
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/industries/restaurants/

| Factor | SBG Funding | Traditional bank | Typical online lender |
| Decision time | Same day | 2 to 8 weeks | 1 to 3 days |
| Credit check | Soft pull only | Hard inquiry | Often a hard pull |
| Minimum credit score | 500+ | 680+ | Varies |
| What is reviewed | Revenue plus 50+ factors, human review | Tax returns and financials | Algorithm only |
| Point of contact | One dedicated advisor | Loan committee | Call center or portal |
| Collateral | None required on unsecured products | Often required | Sometimes |
| Funding range | $5K to $10M | Often a $50K+ minimum | Varies |

Banks rely on rigid credit thresholds and can take weeks. SBG evaluates more than 50 factors, funds in as little as 24 hours and approves businesses whose revenue and time-in-business profiles banks often decline. Most businesses SBG funds have been declined by at least one bank.
