# Qualifying and applying

## Minimum requirements
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

To qualify with SBG Funding a business needs to meet four published minimums:

- Credit score: a business owner FICO score of 500 or higher.
- Time in business: at least 6 months of operating history.
- Revenue: $150,000 or more in gross revenue over the last 12 months (about $12,500 per month).
- Documents: the 4 most recent business bank statements.

Meeting all four means you can apply in about five minutes with no impact to your credit score. SBG evaluates more than 50 factors beyond credit, so strong revenue and consistent deposits can offset a lower score. A business HELOC is the exception: it requires a FICO score of 600 or higher and 6+ months in business, and because it is secured by the owner's home SBG lists no revenue minimum for it.

## Can a new business qualify?
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

Businesses with as little as 6 months of operating history can qualify; SBG looks at recent revenue and bank deposits rather than longevity. Businesses under 6 months old do not yet qualify. If you are in your first 6 months, SBG recommends applying once you cross that threshold with consistent monthly revenue.

## Can I qualify with bad credit?
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/business-financing/bad-credit-business-loans/

Yes. SBG works with FICO scores as low as 500, and bad credit alone will not disqualify an application. Credit influences which products and terms you qualify for, but revenue, deposit consistency and time in business carry significantly more weight. Approvals in the 500 to 600 range rest on the strength of cash flow rather than the score. A FICO below 600 is not a ceiling on the amount; six-figure approvals rest on cash flow.

## What documents do I need to apply?
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

To start, you need only the quick online application and your 4 most recent business bank statements. No tax returns, business plan, projections or audited financials are required to begin. Some products ask for more later:

- SBA 7(a): typically 3 years of business and personal tax returns (for owners with 20%+ ownership), a year-to-date profit and loss statement, bank statements, SBA forms and purpose documents such as a purchase agreement. SBG provides a full checklist after the initial review.
- Equipment financing: details of the equipment (type, price and vendor if available).
- Invoice financing: copies of the outstanding invoices and proof the goods or services were delivered.
- Bridge capital: proof of the repayment source, such as a purchase agreement, commitment letter or contract.
- Business HELOC: home value and mortgage balance to start; later personal tax returns, a mortgage statement and documentation of the home's value.
- Larger amounts may require business tax returns or additional financial documents.

## How the application works
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/

1. Apply online: complete the application in about 5 minutes with basic information about your business and financing needs.
2. Get approved: your advisor reviews the application and presents options matched to your profile, looking at revenue, deposit history and time in business, not just credit.
3. Receive your funds: once approved and documents are signed, funds can be in your business bank account in as little as 24 hours.

## How quickly will I get a decision and funds?
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

Most applications receive a decision the same business day, typically within 24 hours. Applications submitted after business hours, on weekends or on holidays get a response the next business day. Once approved, you can choose a same-day wire or a next-business-day ACH transfer. Same-day funding depends on your qualifications and on returning closing documents promptly. ACH moves only on banking business days. SBA financing is the exception: it typically closes in 30 to 90 days.

## Will applying affect my credit score?
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

No. SBG uses a soft credit pull to verify identity and states that it never performs a hard credit pull at any point in its approval process. Using SBG's online calculators touches no credit bureau at all.

## Which states and industries does SBG serve?
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

SBG Funding finances small businesses in all 50 states, and the application is fully online. SBG finances most industries; the main exceptions are adult entertainment and gambling. If you are unsure whether your business qualifies, call 844-284-2725.

## Is this financing for business or personal use?
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/credit-application-terms/

SBG's financing is for business purposes only. Applicants confirm that the request is for business and not for consumer purposes. SBG does not offer personal loans, auto loans, student loans or home mortgages for personal use.

## What if I already have financing with a bank?
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

SBG's products are designed to work alongside traditional financing. If the business qualifies, SBG can provide funding without disrupting existing obligations. Applicants should avoid stacking several revenue-based advances from different providers at once, because overlapping daily payments are the most common reason businesses run into trouble with that kind of financing.

## Is my information secure?
Source: https://sbgfunding.com/faqs/

SBG Funding states that protecting customer data is a top priority and that it uses secure data-privacy infrastructure that it keeps strengthening as technology evolves. Sensitive details such as Social Security numbers and bank account numbers are collected only through the secure application, never through chat.
